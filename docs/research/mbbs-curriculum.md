Status: research draft. Syllabus/course-list level only; no lecture text copied.

## 0. Method and confidence

Web search was available; direct page fetches (ox.ac.uk/medsci, hopkinsmedicine.org, ucl.ac.uk, education.ki.se) returned 403/406, so course names come from search-result excerpts of the official pages plus general knowledge of published curricula.

- [V] = named in a search excerpt of an official page.
- [S] = synthesised by me from typical content; not copied from one school.
- Duke and Imperial module-level lists were NOT retrieved (Imperial only at programme-description level). Do not cite them for course content until checked.
- All topic lists are [S]. Verify each source URL in a browser before relying on it.

## 2. Design principles

1. Hybrid model: UK schools (Oxford, Cambridge) teach discipline-based science; Karolinska, Hopkins, Harvard, UCL integrate by systems. Recommended: discipline "foundations" in Yr1, systems blocks in Yr2, deepening and research in Yr3.
2. Anatomy early and linked to dissection/prosection (Cambridge FAB, Hopkins 7-week anatomy).
3. Public health, ethics, sociology and clinical skills run longitudinally from week 1 (Oxford Population Health, Hopkins Foundations in Public Health, Karolinska Upptakt).
4. Maps onto the app model (Subject > Topic > Subtopic > Objective): each course = a Subject, each topic = a Topic.

## 3. Course outline

Outcomes are original wording ("the student can ...").

### YEAR 1 - Foundations

#### Semester 1

**1.1 Gross Anatomy I (trunk, thorax, abdomen, pelvis)** - Cambridge FAB; Hopkins Foundations of Human Anatomy; Oxford Organisation of the Body
- Topics: anatomical terminology and planes; back and vertebral column; thoracic wall and cavity; heart and mediastinum; abdominal wall and peritoneum; GI organs and vessels; kidneys and posterior abdomen; pelvis and perineum.
- Subtopics: surface anatomy, dermatomes, coronary supply, hepatic portal system, inguinal canal, pelvic floor.
- Outcomes: name and locate major structures; relate structure to clinical examination; interpret basic radiographs/CT sections; explain common variants.
- URLs: https://www.pdn.cam.ac.uk/undergraduate ; https://www.hopkinsmedicine.org/som/curriculum/genes_to_society/year-one/

**1.2 Histology and Cell Biology** - Cambridge IA Histology; Karolinska Sem 1; Harvard Foundations
- Topics: cell structure and organelles; membranes and transport; cytoskeleton and cell cycle; cell signalling; epithelia and glands; connective tissue, bone, cartilage; muscle and nerve tissue; blood and lymphoid tissue; microscopy and staining.
- Outcomes: identify tissues on slides; link cell structure to function; describe cell-cycle control and its relevance to cancer.
- URLs: https://www.biology.cam.ac.uk/undergrads/MedST/Current/CO ; https://hms.harvard.edu/education-admissions/md-program/curriculum/pathways

**1.3 Medical Biochemistry and Metabolism** - Oxford Biochemistry and Medical Genetics; Cambridge Molecules in Medical Science; Karolinska Sem 1
- Topics: amino acids and proteins; enzymes and kinetics; carbohydrate metabolism; lipid metabolism; bioenergetics and oxidative phosphorylation; nitrogen and urea cycle; nucleotides; integration of metabolism (fed/fasted, diabetes); vitamins and minerals.
- Outcomes: explain major pathways and their regulation; interpret inborn errors and metabolic lab results; connect to diabetes and nutrition.
- URL: https://www.ox.ac.uk/sites/files/oxford/field/field_document/Medicine%20CIS%202026-entry.pdf

**1.4 Molecular Genetics and Embryology** - Oxford Medical Genetics; Harvard Foundations (genetics, developmental biology); Cambridge Human Reproduction
- Topics: DNA structure, replication, repair; transcription and translation; gene regulation and epigenetics; Mendelian and complex inheritance; chromosomal disorders; gametogenesis and fertilisation; weeks 1-3 and germ layers; organogenesis (heart, gut, limbs); congenital anomalies and teratogens.
- Outcomes: draw pedigrees and compute risk; explain origin of common congenital defects; interpret simple genetic tests.
- URL: https://hms.harvard.edu/education-admissions/md-program/curriculum/pathways

**1.5 Introduction to Medicine: Professionalism, Public Health and Ethics I** - Karolinska Upptakt; Hopkins Foundations in Public Health; Oxford Population Health 1, Learning with Patients 1
- Topics: role of the doctor and professionalism; communication and consent basics; four principles of ethics; confidentiality; health determinants and sociology; epidemiology measures; health systems overview; first patient contact and reflection.
- Outcomes: apply an ethical framework to a simple case; compute incidence/prevalence; demonstrate a basic patient interview.
- URLs: https://education.ki.se/programme-syllabus-pdf/2LK13/en ; https://e-catalogue.jhu.edu/medicine/medical-students/md/md.pdf

#### Semester 2

**1.6 Human Physiology I (cardiovascular, respiratory, renal)** - Oxford Physiology and Pharmacology; Cambridge Homeostasis; Hopkins Organ Systems
- Topics: body fluids and homeostasis; cardiac electrophysiology and ECG; cardiac cycle and output; vascular haemodynamics and BP; lung mechanics and gas exchange; control of breathing; renal filtration and tubular function; acid-base and electrolytes; exercise and altitude.
- Outcomes: predict responses to perturbation; interpret ECG, spirometry, blood gas; explain feedback control.
- URL: https://www.medsci.ox.ac.uk/study/medicine/pre-clinical/structure

**1.7 Human Physiology II (GI, endocrine, reproductive, muscle)** - Oxford; Cambridge; Karolinska Den friska manniskan
- Topics: muscle contraction; GI motility and secretion; digestion and absorption; liver and bile; endocrine axes (HPA, thyroid); glucose and calcium regulation; reproductive endocrinology; pregnancy and lactation; thermoregulation.
- Outcomes: explain hormonal feedback loops; interpret basic endocrine tests.
- URL: https://education.ki.se/programme-syllabus-pdf/2LK13/en

**1.8 Gross Anatomy II (limbs and musculoskeletal)** - Cambridge FAB; Oxford Organisation of the Body
- Topics: upper limb bones and joints; brachial plexus and nerves; lower limb bones and joints; lumbosacral plexus; vascular supply and venous drainage; gait and posture; fractures and joint injuries; MSK imaging.
- Outcomes: localise nerve and vessel injuries from signs; describe joint mechanics.
- URL: https://www.pdn.cam.ac.uk/undergraduate

**1.9 Clinical Skills and Learning with Patients I** - Oxford Learning with Patients 1; Hopkins Clinical Foundations
- Topics: history-taking structure; general inspection and vitals; cardiovascular and respiratory examination; abdominal examination; hand hygiene and infection control; BLS/first aid; documentation; communicating with anxious patients.
- Outcomes: perform a structured history and basic systems exam; record findings clearly.
- URL: https://www.hopkinsmedicine.org/som/curriculum/genes_to_society/year-one/

### YEAR 2 - Systems and mechanisms of disease

#### Semester 3

**2.1 Neuroscience and Head and Neck Anatomy** - Oxford The Nervous System; Cambridge Neurobiology and Human Behaviour, Head and Neck Anatomy; Harvard Mind, Brain and Behavior
- Topics: neurons, synapses, neurotransmitters; spinal cord and reflexes; brainstem and cranial nerves; cerebral cortex; basal ganglia and cerebellum; sensory systems (vision, hearing, somatosensory); autonomic nervous system; head and neck anatomy; sleep, memory, pain.
- Outcomes: localise a neurological lesion; describe sensory/motor pathways; interpret basic neuro-imaging.
- URLs: https://www.path.cam.ac.uk/node/134 ; https://meded.hms.harvard.edu/pathways-course-descriptions

**2.2 General Pathology (Principles of Disease)** - Oxford Principles of Pathology; Cambridge Biology of Disease
- Topics: cell injury and death; acute and chronic inflammation; repair and fibrosis; haemodynamics, thrombosis, shock; neoplasia (biology, grading, staging); genetic and developmental disease; ageing; nutritional and environmental pathology; amyloid and deposition diseases.
- Outcomes: explain mechanisms linking injury to disease; recognise common gross/micro patterns; apply staging concepts.
- URLs: https://www.ox.ac.uk/sites/files/oxford/Medicine%202018%20entry.pdf ; https://www.path.cam.ac.uk/node/134

**2.3 Microbiology and Infectious Disease** - UCL Infection and Defence; Harvard Foundations and Immunity in Defense and Disease
- Topics: bacterial structure and growth; gram-positive and gram-negative pathogens; viruses; fungi and parasites; antimicrobial mechanisms and resistance; sterilisation and infection control; sepsis; vaccines; healthcare-associated and global infections.
- Outcomes: link organism to syndrome; choose a logical antimicrobial class; explain resistance.
- URL: https://www.ucl.ac.uk/medical-school/staff-students/course-information/year-2

**2.4 Immunology** - Harvard Immunity in Defense and Disease; UCL Infection and Defence
- Topics: innate immunity and complement; antigen presentation and MHC; T-cell responses; B-cells and antibodies; tolerance and autoimmunity; hypersensitivity; transplantation; immunodeficiency; immunotherapy and vaccines.
- Outcomes: describe the immune response to infection and tumours; classify hypersensitivity; interpret basic serology.
- URL: https://meded.hms.harvard.edu/pathways-course-descriptions

#### Semester 4

**2.5 Pharmacology I (principles)** - Oxford Applied Physiology and Pharmacology; Harvard Foundations
- Topics: pharmacokinetics (ADME); pharmacodynamics and receptors; dose-response and therapeutic index; drug metabolism and interactions; adverse reactions; autonomic pharmacology; prescribing basics and safe use; pharmacogenomics.
- Outcomes: calculate basic PK parameters; explain mechanism-to-effect; identify high-risk drugs and interactions.
- URL: https://www.ox.ac.uk/sites/files/oxford/Medicine%202018%20entry.pdf

**2.6 Integrated Organ-System Pathophysiology** - Hopkins Organ Systems Foundations; UCL Yr2 systems modules; Harvard Integrated Human Pathophysiology
- Topics: cardiovascular (IHD, HF, arrhythmia, hypertension); respiratory (asthma, COPD, ILD, lung cancer); renal and fluid disorders; GI and liver; endocrine and metabolic; haematology; musculoskeletal and rheumatology; dermatology basics; reproductive and breast disease.
- Each block integrates anatomy, physiology, pathology, pharmacology and a clinical case.
- Outcomes: explain presentations from mechanism; propose first-line investigations; interpret key lab values.
- URLs: https://www.hopkinsmedicine.org/som/curriculum/genes_to_society/year-one/ ; https://www.ucl.ac.uk/medical-school/staff-students/course-information/year-2

**2.7 Psychology, Sociology and Behavioural Science** - Oxford Psychology for Medicine; Harvard Mind, Brain and Behavior
- Topics: cognition and perception; learning and behaviour change; stress, coping, illness behaviour; mood and anxiety disorders; psychosis; substance use; health beliefs and adherence; mental capacity and consent.
- Outcomes: apply behaviour-change models; recognise major psychiatric presentations.
- URL: https://www.ox.ac.uk/sites/files/oxford/Medicine%202018%20entry.pdf

**2.8 Clinical Skills and Patient and Doctor II** - Oxford Patient and Doctor; Hopkins Clinical Foundations
- Topics: neurological and MSK examination; breaking bad news; intro to clinical reasoning; prescribing and drug charts; venepuncture/injection practice; safeguarding; multidisciplinary teamwork; case presentation.
- Outcomes: present a patient concisely; carry out additional examinations safely.
- URL: https://www.ox.ac.uk/sites/files/oxford/Medicine%202018%20entry.pdf

### YEAR 3 - Integration, research, transition to clinical

#### Semester 5

**3.1 Advanced Pathology and Clinical Pharmacology** - Oxford Yr3 BA options; Cambridge Part II; Harvard Integrated Human Pathophysiology
- Topics: organ-focused systemic pathology; cancer biology and oncology principles; diagnostic histopathology and cytology; chemical pathology and laboratory medicine; clinical pharmacology by drug class; pharmacovigilance; evidence-based prescribing; toxicology and overdose.
- Outcomes: interpret pathology reports; justify drug choices from guidelines; handle adverse-event reporting.
- URL: https://www.medsci.ox.ac.uk/study/medicine/pre-clinical/structure

**3.2 Public Health, Epidemiology and Biostatistics** - Hopkins Foundations in Public Health; Oxford Population Health; Stanford Science of Medicine
- Topics: study designs; bias and confounding; screening and diagnostic accuracy; hypothesis testing and CI; regression and survival analysis (intro); systematic reviews; global health and burden of disease; health-care systems and policy; environmental and occupational health.
- Outcomes: critically appraise a paper; compute sensitivity/specificity/NNT; discuss population interventions.
- URLs: https://e-catalogue.jhu.edu/medicine/medical-students/md/md.pdf ; https://med.stanford.edu/md/mdhandbook/section-4--discovery-curriculum-overview/section-4-5-required-pre-clerkship-courses.html

**3.3 Medical Ethics, Law and Professionalism** - Hopkins Genes to Society; UCL Foundations of Health and Medical Practice
- Topics: consent and capacity; confidentiality and data protection; end-of-life care; reproductive ethics; research ethics; resource allocation; whistleblowing and duty of candour; equality, diversity, health disparities.
- Outcomes: analyse a case with a structured ethical/legal approach; recognise professional duties.
- URLs: https://www.hopkinsmedicine.org/som/curriculum/genes_to_society/year-one/ ; https://www.ucl.ac.uk/module-catalogue/modules/foundations-of-health-and-medical-practice-MBBS0013

#### Semester 6

**3.4 Research Project / Scholarly Concentration** - Oxford BA research project; Hopkins Scholarly Concentrations; Stanford Discovery curriculum
- Topics: formulating a question; literature search; study design; data collection; analysis; scientific writing; poster/oral presentation; research integrity.
- Outcomes: complete a supervised project and present results.
- URLs: https://www.hopkinsmedicine.org/som/curriculum/genes_to_society/year-one/ ; https://med.stanford.edu/news/all-news/2018/09/new-med-school-curriculum-expands-opportunities-for-research.html

**3.5 Clinical Anatomy and Imaging (consolidation)** - Oxford Principles of Anatomy (end Yr3)
- Topics: radiographic anatomy; CT/MRI/ultrasound anatomy; surgical approaches; procedural anatomy (lumbar puncture, central lines); clinical anatomy case sets; embryological basis of anomalies.
- Outcomes: apply anatomy to procedures and imaging.
- URL: https://www.ox.ac.uk/sites/files/oxford/field/field_document/Medicine%20CIS%202026-entry.pdf

**3.6 Transition to Clinical Practice** - Harvard BRIDGES; Hopkins Clinical Foundations
- Topics: clinical reasoning and differential diagnosis; ward etiquette and handover; prescribing safety; recognising deterioration; patient safety and quality improvement; shadowing placements; wellbeing and resilience.
- Outcomes: demonstrate readiness for clerkships.
- URL: https://meded.hms.harvard.edu/pathways-course-descriptions
